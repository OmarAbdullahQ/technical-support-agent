# Billing Support Knowledge Base

This document contains trusted support guidance for the `billing` intent.

## Scope

Use this document for requests classified as `billing`.

The guidance is intended for retrieval and extractive QA.

## Invoices

1. Use the billing system as the source of truth for invoice status.
2. Verify invoice numbers before investigating a billing case.
3. A payment can be pending, completed, failed, reversed, or refunded.
4. Do not request full card numbers in support messages.
5. A successful authorization does not always mean payment capture succeeded.
6. Refund timing depends on the provider and issuing bank.
7. Subscription charges depend on the active plan and billing cycle.
8. Prorated charges may occur after a plan change.
9. Cancellation does not automatically imply a refund.
10. Account credits are different from cash refunds.
