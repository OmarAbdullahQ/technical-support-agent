# Complaint Support Knowledge Base

This document contains trusted support guidance for the `complaint` intent.

## Scope

Use this document for requests classified as `complaint`.

The guidance is intended for retrieval and extractive QA.

## Complaint intake

1. Acknowledge complaints clearly and professionally.
2. Capture the main concern in neutral language.
3. Separate facts from assumptions.
4. Record relevant dates, ticket IDs, and transaction IDs.
5. Do not dismiss a complaint because it cannot be reproduced immediately.
6. Avoid blaming the customer.
7. Do not promise an outcome before investigation.
8. Check incident records for service quality complaints.
9. Support behavior complaints should be handled separately from the technical issue.
10. Collect version and reproduction steps for product complaints.

## Service quality complaints

11. Distinguish feature requests from confirmed defects.
12. Check account records before confirming a billing complaint.
