# Cancellation Support Knowledge Base

This document contains trusted support guidance for the `cancellation` intent.

## Scope

Use this document for requests classified as `cancellation`.

The guidance is intended for retrieval and extractive QA.

## Cancellation requests

1. Confirm the customer intends to cancel before changing account state.
2. A cancellation may be immediate or effective at the end of the billing period.
3. Record the cancellation request timestamp.
4. Cancellation is different from a refund request.
5. A canceled subscription may remain active until the paid period ends.
6. Do not cancel additional services unless the customer requested them.
7. A pause is not the same as a cancellation.
8. Do not pressure customers to stay subscribed.
9. Retention offers should be documented and authorized.
10. Refund eligibility should be evaluated separately.
